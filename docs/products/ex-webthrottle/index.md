**EX‑WebThrottle** is a web based throttle (controller) app that can connect to an **EX-CommandStation** (ready to run [EX-CSB1](../ex-csb1/index.md) or [self built](../../diy/diy.md)) directly through the USB port of a computer.

Unlike [other throttle (controller) apps](../../throttles/throttles.md), **EX-WebThrottle** has been developed by the the **DCC-EX** team.

Read the [following pages](ex-webthrottle.md) on how to connect and use **EX-WebThrottle** to control your trains and accessories, plus read and write CVs to your DCC decoders.
