# Selecting a power supply

You will require a power supply unit to provide track power and to run the **EX-CSB1** processor.

If you have purchased an optional extra **EX-MotorShield8874** for your **EX-CSB1**, then you will need *a second power supply*. One to Power the **EX-CSB1** and one to power the **EX-MotorShield8874**.

Consult with your supplier when purchasing your **EX-CSB1** and/or **EX-MotorShield8874** as to what power supply you should use, and use the details below for reference.
