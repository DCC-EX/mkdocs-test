# Connecting to your layout

Once you have purchased or built your **EX-CommandStation**, you will need o to connect it to your layout and programming track to test it and to run your trains.

Depending on whether or not you purchased an **EX-CSB1** or you self-built your own **EX-CommandStation**, the instructions for connecting to your layout and programming track will be slightly different.

- See the [Connecting your **EX-CSB1**](connecting-ex-csb1.md) page if you have purchased an **EX-CSB1**.
- See the [Connecting your self-built **EX-CommandStation**](connecting-diy.md) page if you built your own.
